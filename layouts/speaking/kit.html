Linked from the invitation at the top of the speaking page, which is the one place the speaking page has prose. Three parts, in the order a call-for-papers form asks for them: the facts (name, role, topics, contact), a short bio and a long one, then photos. The bios come from front matter, `bio.short` and `bio.long`, and each is labelled with its length in words, counted here, because calls for papers cap a bio by words and an organiser should not have to count. The photos are files in the page bundle, listed in `photos` (src, name, label, alt). Each is offered as a JPEG, converted here if the file is not one, since upload forms still turn away WebP; its size is read from the file, so the kit cannot describe a photo as larger than it is.
