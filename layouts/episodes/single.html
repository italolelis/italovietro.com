{{- /* A podcast appearance on its own page: the episode chapter by chapter, with a short summary, the quotes that carry it and the lesson pulled out. The pages live in content/episodes/ and are reached from their entries on the speaking page. Claude writes them, from a transcript of the recording, and the page says so before anything else: a disclaimer under the masthead, the author in the metadata, the summaries in the third person. The quotes are the only words on the page that are Italo's. That is also why they are not in content/posts/ -- the writing section is only what he writes -- and why they are not in the feeds or the tag pages. Everything below the masthead comes from front matter (`chapters[].summary`, `.moments`, `.lesson`, `.plate`); the Markdown body is only the short intro. A full long-read format was built for the first episode and dropped in favour of this: ten times the words to maintain in two languages, for a page most readers skim for the lessons. On top of the chapters: a masthead, the chapter list, a colophon, and the timeline pinned under the header, which marks every quote on the page. A chapter can also ask the reader something before it answers it (`ask`, partials/episode/ask.html) and carry an explanatory figure after its summary (`figures`, partials/episode/figure.html).
